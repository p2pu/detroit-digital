---
title: "Program Requirements"
---
For learners to complete the program successfully they need to complete 15 hours of digital literacy training some of this time can be accounted for by homework, or practice time ~ 3 hours of out of class time is acceptable. During the 15 hours of digital literacy training the core modules Basic Computer Skills and Online Navigation must be completed.  If students miss class days it is at the facilitator’s discretion to provide a make up day.  All learners will need to complete the device agreement form.
